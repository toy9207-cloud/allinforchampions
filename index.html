Our mission

No young champion fights alone.

All In For Champions brings together the rodeo community, National Finals Rodeo contestants and generous sponsors for an unforgettable event benefiting Golden Circle of Champions. Through the excitement of competition and the power of giving, we go all in for children and families facing childhood cancer.

All In For Champions is a nonprofit public charity. 100% of the money raised goes to Golden Circle of Champions.

The tournament

NFR Celebrity Blackjack

Held each year during the Wrangler National Finals Rodeo, the tournament brings together members of the rodeo, Western and entertainment communities for a morning of friendly competition and giving.

Ten NFR contestants and five VIP players take a seat at the table, with every buy-in covered by a sponsor. Lefty Holman emcees, and The Cowboy Channel will film the event.

Friends, family and fans are welcome to come watch and cheer.
